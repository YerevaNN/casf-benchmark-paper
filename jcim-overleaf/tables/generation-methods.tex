\par\smallskip
\noindent For PLINDER, torsion sampling starts from one ETKDGv3 conformer built from
isomeric SMILES, without experimental coordinates. Raw and minimized pools are sampled
separately from that geometry, each with 1,000 retained conformers. The minimized pipeline
applies clash checks before and after MMFF94s optimization and rejects stereochemical
mismatches. In contrast, PLINDER RDKit minimization operates on the saved raw conformers
in their original order. Both use up to 500 optimization iterations and permit outputs
that reach the iteration limit. Classical-pool validity checks use atom ordering matched
to the reference topology; PoseBusters is not applied as a coverage or clustering filter.
