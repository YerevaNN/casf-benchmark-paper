$n$ is the number of nonempty ensembles with defined energies. Energy SD is the population standard deviation within each molecule's ensemble; its median and mean are then taken across molecules. Mean energy is likewise calculated within each ensemble before taking the median. Paired mean-energy differences are calculated against the stored ChEMBL3D ensemble for the same molecule before aggregation; positive values indicate higher mean energy under this force field. They are not differences of aggregate medians or binding energies.

RDKit 2025.09.6 was used with MMFF94s. Hydrogens were removed and rebuilt with assigned coordinates before single-point energy evaluation. Neither hydrogen nor heavy-atom coordinates were optimized.

All 77,986 retained conformers produced finite energies, with no heavy-atom displacement. The three empty method--molecule outputs (two NExT-Mol and one MCF) have no defined energy distribution and are excluded from these means and medians. Per-molecule and per-conformer records are archived with the analysis. Taking a median across molecules does not remove extreme structures from the ensembles.
