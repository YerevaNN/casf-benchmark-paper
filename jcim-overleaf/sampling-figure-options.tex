The sampling curves show a similar transition in both CASF panels
(Figure~\ref{fig:sampling-option-b1}): LoQI leads these four methods at the smallest budget,
while Qwen overtakes it as the budget increases. The ordering also depends on the matching
criterion and dataset. At the largest budget, LoQI reaches 80\% recovery at a tighter RMSD
threshold than Qwen on core, whereas Qwen reaches it at the tightest threshold on ref.
A ranking at one budget and tolerance therefore does not fully describe sampling performance.
