Across intermediate budgets, the relative performance of the generators changes
(Figure~\ref{fig:sampling-option-b1}). LoQI has the highest recovery among these four methods at
small budgets, while Qwen overtakes it as the budget increases. At 1,000 candidates, Qwen recovers
91.5\% of entries at 0.75 \AA{}, compared with 86.2\% for LoQI, FlowR, and NExT-Mol. When recovery
is fixed at 80\%, LoQI reaches this target at a slightly tighter RMSD threshold than Qwen at the
largest budget, 0.479 versus 0.506 \AA{}. The ordering therefore depends on both the sampling budget
and the required proximity to the experimental conformation.
