The sampling curves show a similar transition in both CASF panels
(Figure~\ref{fig:sampling-budget}C,D): LoQI leads these four methods at the smallest budget,
while Qwen overtakes it as the budget increases. Qwen continues to gain recovery
beyond the mean ChEMBL conformer count in both panels. On PLINDER, LoQI's coverage
advantage narrows as sampling increases, but remains at the largest budget
(Figure~\ref{fig:sampling-budget}A). Matching distance shows a different ordering:
LoQI has lower MAT-R at small budgets, whereas Qwen reaches the lowest MAT-R at
1,000 conformers (Figure~\ref{fig:sampling-budget}B).
