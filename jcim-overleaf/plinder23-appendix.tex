\section*{Selection and composition of the PLINDER-22 panel}

The evaluated panel derives from an exploratory, manually curated selection from PLINDER
release \textbf{2024-06/v2}, rather than an official PLINDER split. Selection began with the
ligand-per-system table, grouped by complete InChIKey to retain stereoisomers as separate
identities. The starting collection contained \textbf{51,280 identities}; the physicochemical
requirements in Table~S5 retained \textbf{25,392 (49.5\%)}.

Surviving identities were ranked by descending number of distinct PDB entries, with system
count as the secondary ordering. Manual review selected recognizable drugs and
medicinal-chemistry-like protein ligands, excluding common crystallization additives,
metabolites, cofactors, and other compounds outside the intended panel. These were
judgment-based categories rather than executable exclusion rules. The original selection
contained 23 molecules, including bortezomib; the evaluated cleaned set excludes bortezomib
because of covalent annotations.

The panel is not a random sample or simply the most frequent PLINDER ligands. A later screen
based on DrugBank-derived ATC annotations did not define it, and neither an ATC requirement
nor a minimum of ten distinct PDB entries should be inferred. Exact reproduction uses the
saved molecular identities. Training exclusions are a separate operation from panel construction.

The supplied cleaned structures were deduplicated at 0.05~\AA{} symmetry-aware RMSD and
aligned with a fixed anchor atom triplet per molecule. The delivered 22-molecule collection
contained 1,070 representatives. Inspection of stereochemistry from the coordinates identified
two inverted chloramphenicol records from PDB 2uxp and one staurosporine epimer from PDB 2oic.
Removing those three records leaves \textbf{1,067 references}, with \textbf{8--117 per molecule}
and a median of \textbf{40}. Original record identifiers are retained; gaps are intentional.

The retained references span \textbf{552 distinct PDB entries} and \textbf{1,045 PLINDER systems}.
A system can contain multiple ligand instances, so its identifier is not a unique conformer
key. Each record is identified by its CCD code and original conformer index. Duplicate-cluster
membership accounts for 2,258 original records after stereochemical exclusions, but each
retained representative receives equal weight within its molecule. Pleconaril illustrates the
effect of deduplication: 716 original records reduce to eight representatives. Reference counts
are not counts of distinct protein targets or biologically distinct conformational states.

Molecular descriptors are retained from the saved identity shortlist. The companion
\texttt{plinder22-molecules.csv} gives molecular identities and descriptors, original selection
frequencies, and updated coordinate counts in separate columns. Tables~S6--S7 describe the
cleaned collection rather than the original selection frequencies or current PDB contents.
Source hashes and the per-record reference manifest are archived in \texttt{plinder22-data/}.
