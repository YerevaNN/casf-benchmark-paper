\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

Qwen provides broad geometric sampling and strong large-budget recovery, whereas
LoQI concentrates more samples near the available PLINDER references. Both learned
sources substantially exceed RDKit in the final large-budget pharmacophore comparison,
but intervals for most increments of 50:50 over the stronger pure source include zero,
and the data do not reliably distinguish 50:50 from 75:25 at the intended larger
budgets. We therefore select 50\% Qwen and 50\% LoQI as a simple fixed production
policy with balanced source representation, not as a uniquely established optimum.
Protein-informed queries support the learned-versus-RDKit separation and assess
binding-site relevance, while ligand-only evidence leads the reusable-library choice.
Downstream docking, screening, and learning evaluations remain necessary before
assigning the resulting resource an application-specific advantage.
